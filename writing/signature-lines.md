# Signature lines

Lines Nic has said or written that are hers, kept so they stop depending on memory. Use them sparingly and only where they fit; a line that appears in every piece stops being a line. Several have a written form and a spoken form, and they are not always the same words.

Companion to [voice-spec-template.md](voice-spec-template.md) (the "Signature phrases" section) and [ai-writing-style-guide.md](ai-writing-style-guide.md). The test for adding a line: she said it, in that shape, and it sounds like her. Lines drafted by someone else and adopted stay out until she has reworked them.

Last reviewed: 2026-10-06.

## A thought, not a one-liner

> Carelessness is a routine that's been installed in us without our permission. Our attention hasn't just been depleted, it's been hijacked.

Two sentences, effect then cause. The argument behind it (attention and alarm as evolved instruments, the attention economy as supernormal stimulus, automation bias, verification as routine because vigilance is a depleted resource) belongs to the Thinking is Critical course, not here.
